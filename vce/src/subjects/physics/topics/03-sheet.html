In the Physics exam you get the VCAA formula sheet, and you can also bring pre-written notes: one folded A3 sheet or two A4 sheets bound together by tape. Add blocks on the left and they pack into columns in order, at real paper size. The meter shows how full each side is. Tap any block to rewrite it, and drag blocks to reorder them. Everything saves in this browser.

Don't spend space on what the formula sheet already gives you (those blocks are tagged VCAA). Use it for derived results, traps, methods and diagrams. Tips: your notes sheet.
