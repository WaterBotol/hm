Definitions written the way VCAA uses them. Click the link after each for the full explanation.

A–F

Arc length
The length of a curve: \(\int\sqrt{1 + (y')^2}\,dx\) or \(\int\sqrt{\dot x^2 + \dot y^2}\,dt\). Arc length
Argand diagram
The complex plane: real part on the horizontal axis, imaginary part on the vertical. Complex numbers
Argument (Arg)
The angle of a complex number from the positive real axis; the principal argument \(\operatorname{Arg} z \in (-\pi, \pi]\). Complex numbers
Central limit theorem
For large \(n\), the sample mean is approximately normal with mean \(\mu\) and sd \(\frac{\sigma}{\sqrt n}\), whatever the population shape. Sample means
Collinear
Lying on one straight line: \(\overrightarrow{AB} = k\overrightarrow{AC}\). Vectors
Confidence interval
An interval \(\bar x \pm z\frac{\sigma}{\sqrt n}\) produced by a method that captures \(\mu\) in C% of samples. Confidence intervals
Conjugate
\(\bar z = x - yi\) for \(z = x + yi\); reflection in the real axis. Complex numbers
Conjugate root theorem
A polynomial with real coefficients has non-real roots in conjugate pairs. Complex polynomials
Contrapositive
For \(P \Rightarrow Q\), the statement \(\lnot Q \Rightarrow \lnot P\); logically equivalent to the original. Logic
Converse
For \(P \Rightarrow Q\), the statement \(Q \Rightarrow P\); not equivalent in general. Logic
Counterexample
One specific case showing a universal statement is false. Proof
Cross (vector) product
\(\underset{\sim}{a}\times\underset{\sim}{b}\): a vector perpendicular to both with magnitude \(|\underset{\sim}{a}||\underset{\sim}{b}|\sin\theta\). Cross product & planes
De Moivre's theorem
\((r\operatorname{cis}\theta)^n = r^n\operatorname{cis}(n\theta)\). De Moivre
Differential equation
An equation involving an unknown function and its derivatives. DEs
Displacement / distance
Displacement \(\int v\,dt\) is signed change in position; distance \(\int|v|\,dt\) is total path length. Kinematics
Dot (scalar) product
\(\underset{\sim}{a}\cdot\underset{\sim}{b} = |\underset{\sim}{a}||\underset{\sim}{b}|\cos\theta\); zero for perpendicular vectors. Vectors
Equilibrium solution
A constant solution of a DE, where \(\frac{dy}{dx} = 0\) for all \(x\). Solving DEs
Euler's method
Numerical approximation \(y_{n+1} = y_n + hf(x_n, y_n)\). DEs & Euler

G–N

Hypothesis test
A procedure deciding whether sample data give evidence against \(H_0: \mu = \mu_0\). Hypothesis tests
Implicit differentiation
Differentiating both sides of a relation in \(x\) and \(y\), treating \(y\) as a function of \(x\). Implicit differentiation
Induction
Proving \(P(n)\) for all \(n \ge n_0\) by a base case and the step \(P(k) \Rightarrow P(k + 1)\). Induction
Linear combination
An expression \(a\underset{\sim}{u} + b\underset{\sim}{v}\) (vectors) or \(aX + bY\) (random variables). Linear combinations
Linearly dependent
A set of vectors where one is a linear combination of the others. Vectors
Logistic equation
\(\frac{dP}{dt} = rP\left(1 - \frac PK\right)\): growth limited by carrying capacity \(K\). Solving DEs
Margin of error
Half the width of a confidence interval: \(z\frac{\sigma}{\sqrt n}\). Confidence intervals
Modulus
\(|z| = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2}\), the distance from the origin. Complex numbers
Normal (to a plane)
A vector perpendicular to every vector in the plane. Cross product & planes
Null hypothesis
\(H_0\): the default claim, \(\mu = \mu_0\). Hypothesis tests

O–Z

p-value
The probability, assuming \(H_0\), of a sample mean at least as extreme as the one observed. Hypothesis tests
Parametric equations
\(x\) and \(y\) (and \(z\)) given as functions of a parameter \(t\). Vector curves
Partial fractions
Writing a rational function as a sum of simpler fractions with linear or quadratic denominators. Partial fractions
Proof by contradiction
Assume the negation of the statement and derive a contradiction. Proof
Quantifiers
\(\forall\) ("for all") and \(\exists\) ("there exists"); negation swaps them. Logic
Root of unity
A solution of \(z^n = 1\): \(\operatorname{cis}\frac{2k\pi}{n}\). De Moivre
Scalar / vector resolute
The component of \(\underset{\sim}{a}\) along \(\underset{\sim}{b}\): \(\underset{\sim}{a}\cdot\hat{\underset{\sim}{b}}\), and that times \(\hat{\underset{\sim}{b}}\). Vectors
Separable DE
\(\frac{dy}{dx} = f(x)g(y)\), solved by \(\int\frac{dy}{g(y)} = \int f(x)\,dx\). Solving DEs
Skew lines
Lines in 3D that are neither parallel nor intersecting. Cross product & planes
Slope field
A grid of short segments with gradient \(\frac{dy}{dx}\) showing the shape of solutions. DEs
Speed
The magnitude of velocity, \(|\underset{\sim}{v}|\). Vector calculus
Surface area of revolution
\(2\pi\int y\,ds\) for rotation about the \(x\)-axis. Arc length
Terminal velocity
The limiting velocity where acceleration is zero. Kinematics
Type I / type II error
Rejecting a true \(H_0\) / failing to reject a false \(H_0\). Hypothesis tests
Volume of revolution
\(\pi\int y^2\,dx\) (about \(x\)-axis) or \(\pi\int x^2\,dy\) (about \(y\)-axis). Areas & volumes
