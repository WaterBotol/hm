2023 VCAA Exam 2 · Section A · Question 17 Open in the exam PDF (p. 8) ↗

Find α, β, γ from \(\underset{\sim}{a}\times\underset{\sim}{b} = \underset{\sim}{c}\)

\(\underset{\sim}{a}\times\underset{\sim}{b} = (4 + \beta)\underset{\sim}{i} - (4\alpha + 3)\underset{\sim}{j} + (\alpha\beta - 3)\underset{\sim}{k}\). Matching gives \(\beta = -2\), \(\alpha = 1\), \(\gamma = -5\).

2024 VCAA Exam 2 · Section A · Question 3 Open in the exam PDF (p. 2) ↗

Values of h for which \(\frac{x - h}{(x + 1)(x - 4)}\) has no turning points

The numerator of \(f^{\prime}\) is \(-x^2 + 2hx - 4 - 3h\); no real roots needs \(h^2 - 3h - 4 < 0\). At \(h = -1\) or \(4\) the function cancels to a reciprocal with no turning points, so \(-1 \le h \le 4\).

70% of Victorian students got this right. Choices: A 13% · B 11% · C 70% · D 6%.

Separating variables in \(\frac{dy}{dx} = e^{x - y}(\cos(x - y) - \cos(x + y))\)

\(\operatorname{Re}\left(\frac{1}{1 - z}\right)\) when \(|z| = 1\)

With \(z = \operatorname{cis}\theta\), \(\frac{1}{1 - z} = \frac{1 - \bar z}{|1 - z|^2}\) and \(|1 - z|^2 = 2 - 2\cos\theta\), giving real part \(\frac12\).

2025 VCAA Exam 2 · Section A · Question 7 Open in the exam PDF (p. 4) ↗

Rewriting a trig integral with the substitution u = cos θ

\(\frac12\sin 2\theta = \sin\theta\cos\theta\); with \(du = -\sin\theta\,d\theta\) the terminals flip to \(0 \to 1\): \(\int_0^1\frac{u}{1 + u}du = \int_0^1\left(1 - \frac{1}{1 + u}\right)du\).
