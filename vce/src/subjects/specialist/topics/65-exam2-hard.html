2023 VCAA Exam 2 · Section A · Question 5 Open in the exam PDF (p. 3) ↗

Given \(|\bar z| = 4\), \(\arg(z^3) = -\pi\) and z in the first quadrant, identify \(z^2\)

\(\arg z \in (0, \frac\pi2)\) and \(3\arg z\) must be \(\pi\), so \(z = 4\operatorname{cis}\frac\pi3\) and \(z^2 = 16\operatorname{cis}\frac{2\pi}{3}\). That equals \(-4\bar z\).

34% of Victorian students got this right. Choices: A 15% · B 20% · C 13% · D 18% · E 34%. The most popular wrong answer was B (20%).

2023 VCAA Exam 2 · Section A · Question 8 Open in the exam PDF (p. 5) ↗

Mixing problem in a spa pool with unequal inflow and outflow rates

Volume \(8000 - 5t\); outflow \(20\cdot\frac{Q}{8000 - 5t} = \frac{4Q}{1600 - t}\), so \(\frac{dQ}{dt} = -\frac{4Q}{1600 - t} = \frac{4Q}{t - 1600}\). The popular wrong answer dropped the minus sign.

37% of Victorian students got this right. Choices: A 37% · B 6% · C 13% · D 14% · E 29%. The most popular wrong answer was E (29%).

Reduction formula for \(I_n = \int_0^1 (1 - x)^n e^x\,dx\)

2025 VCAA Exam 2 · Section A · Question 12 Open in the exam PDF (p. 7) ↗

Velocity at the midpoint under constant acceleration
