Rational function \(f(x) = \frac{x^2 + x - 6}{x - 1}\): rewrite by division, then sketch with asymptotes

State average for the whole question: 2.8 / 4 (70%).

Show \(f(x) = x + 2 - \frac{4}{x - 1}\)

  • Division: \(x^2 + x - 6 = (x - 1)(x + 2) - 4\)

\(x^2 + x - 6 = (x - 1)(x + 2) - 4\), so divide through by \(x - 1\).

State average 0.9 / 1 (90%); 87% scored full marks.

Mark distribution: 0: 13% · 1: 87%

Sketch the graph, labelling asymptotes

  • Asymptotes \(x = 1\) and \(y = x + 2\) drawn and labelled
  • Branches approach both asymptotes (correct shape in each region)
  • Passes through \((-3, 0)\), \((2, 0)\) and \((0, 6)\)

Asymptotes \(x = 1\), \(y = x + 2\); intercepts \((-3, 0)\), \((2, 0)\), \((0, 6)\). Examiners saw graphs drifting away from the asymptotes and missing oblique asymptotes.

State average 1.9 / 3 (63%); 39% scored full marks.

Mark distribution: 0: 18% · 1: 20% · 2: 23% · 3: 39%

2023 VCAA Exam 1 · Question 5 Open in the exam PDF (p. 5) ↗

Evaluate \(\int_1^2 x^2\log_e x\,dx\)

State average for the whole question: 2.0 / 3 (67%).

Evaluate the integral

  • Parts with \(u = \log_e x\), \(v^{\prime} = x^2\): \(\left[\frac{x^3}{3}\log_e x\right]_1^2 - \int_1^2\frac{x^2}{3}dx\)
  • \(\int_1^2\frac{x^2}{3}dx = \frac79\)
  • \(\frac83\log_e 2 - \frac79\)

\(\frac83\log_e 2 - \frac79\). Some students chose \(u\) and \(v^{\prime}\) the wrong way round.

State average 2 / 3 (67%); 53% scored full marks.

Mark distribution: 0: 23% · 1: 10% · 2: 14% · 3: 53%

2023 VCAA Exam 1 · Question 6 Open in the exam PDF (p. 6) ↗

Sum of three independent normal travel times; probability statement for a 12-day average waiting time

State average for the whole question: 2.6 / 4 (65%).

Mean and sd of the total time

  • Mean \(20 + 8 + 12 = 40\)
  • sd \(= \sqrt{36 + 3 + 25} = 8\)

Mean 40 min, sd 8 min. Add variances, not standard deviations.

State average 1.7 / 2 (85%); 75% scored full marks.

Mark distribution: 0: 6% · 1: 19% · 2: 75%

Values \(a, b\) for the 12-day average

  • \(\operatorname{sd}(\bar X_w) = \frac{\sqrt3}{\sqrt{12}} = \frac12\)
  • \(a = -\frac12,\ b = 1\)

7.75 and 8.5 min standardise to \(a = -0.5\), \(b = 1\) (by symmetry \(a = -1\), \(b = 0.5\) also works). The common error was using \(\sqrt3\) instead of \(\frac{\sqrt3}{\sqrt{12}}\).

State average 0.9 / 2 (45%); 40% scored full marks.

Mark distribution: 0: 47% · 1: 13% · 2: 40%

2024 VCAA Exam 1 · Question 2 Open in the exam PDF (p. 3) ↗

Direct proof that \(2x^2 - 3x - 7\) is even whenever \(x\) is odd

State average for the whole question: 2.5 / 3 (83%).

Direct proof

  • Lets \(x = 2k + 1\), \(k \in Z\)
  • Expands to \(8k^2 + 2k - 8\)
  • \(= 2(4k^2 + k - 4)\), a multiple of 2, so even

\(2(2k + 1)^2 - 3(2k + 1) - 7 = 2(4k^2 + k - 4)\), which is even.

State average 2.5 / 3 (83%); 65% scored full marks.

Mark distribution: 0: 8% · 1: 4% · 2: 23% · 3: 65%

Angle between two vectors; integer n for which a dot product equals the magnitude of a cross product

State average for the whole question: 2.9 / 4 (72%).

Angle between \(\underset{\sim}{a}\) and \(\underset{\sim}{b}\)

  • \(\cos\theta = \frac{-9}{3\sqrt2\times3} = -\frac{1}{\sqrt2}\)
  • \(\theta = \frac{3\pi}{4}\)

\(\frac{3\pi}{4}\) (135°). Many found \(\cos\theta\) correctly but then gave \(\frac\pi4\).

State average 1.4 / 2 (70%); 45% scored full marks.

Values of \(n\)

  • \(n = \pm2\)

\(81 = 9 + 18n^2\), so \(n = \pm 2\). Give both values.

State average 1.5 / 2 (75%); 60% scored full marks.

2024 VCAA Exam 1 · Question 5 Open in the exam PDF (p. 7) ↗

Volume of revolution of \(y = \sqrt{k - \frac{1}{x^2}}\) leads to a cubic in \(k\)

State average for the whole question: 2.1 / 3 (70%).

Show k satisfies the cubic

  • \(V = \pi\int_1^{k/2}\left(k - \frac{1}{x^2}\right)dx\)
  • \(= \pi\left(\frac{k^2}{2} + \frac2k - k - 1\right)\)
  • Sets equal to \(\frac{7\pi}{2}\) and rearranges to \(k^3 - 2k^2 - 9k + 4 = 0\)

\(\pi\left[kx + \frac1x\right]_1^{k/2} = \frac{7\pi}{2}\), then multiply by \(2k\). Some students used a surface-area formula by mistake.

State average 2.1 / 3 (70%); 59% scored full marks.

Mark distribution: 0: 22% · 1: 11% · 2: 9% · 3: 59%

2025 VCAA Exam 1 · Question 1 Open in the exam PDF (p. 2) ↗

Tangent to \(xe^{-2y} + y^2e^x = 8e^4\) at \((4, -2)\)

State average for the whole question: 2.9 / 4 (72%).

Equation of the tangent

  • Implicit differentiation: \(e^{-2y} - 2xe^{-2y}\frac{dy}{dx} + 2ye^x\frac{dy}{dx} + y^2e^x = 0\)
  • Substitutes \((4, -2)\): \(5e^4 - 12e^4\frac{dy}{dx} = 0\)
  • \(\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{5}{12}\)
  • \(y = \frac{5}{12}x - \frac{11}{3}\)

\(y + 2 = \frac{5}{12}(x - 4)\), i.e. \(y = \frac{5x}{12} - \frac{11}{3}\). Arithmetic slips in the gradient cost most marks.

State average 2.9 / 4 (72%); 48% scored full marks.

Mark distribution: 0: 10% · 1: 3% · 2: 27% · 3: 12% · 4: 48%

Quartic \(z^4 + 6z^2 + 25\): plot \(1 + 2i\) and its conjugate, a quadratic factor, the remaining roots

State average for the whole question: 3.3 / 5 (66%).

Plot and label \(z_1\) and \(\bar z_1\)

  • \((1, 2)\) and \((1, -2)\) plotted and labelled

\(1 + 2i\) and \(1 - 2i\).

State average 0.8 / 1 (80%); 84% scored full marks.

Mark distribution: 0: 16% · 1: 84%

A quadratic factor

  • Conjugate \(1 - 2i\) is also a root
  • \(z^2 - 2z + 5\)

\((z - 1 - 2i)(z - 1 + 2i) = z^2 - 2z + 5\).

State average 1.4 / 2 (70%); 61% scored full marks.

Mark distribution: 0: 19% · 1: 20% · 2: 61%

Remaining solutions

  • Other factor \(z^2 + 2z + 5\)
  • \(z = -1 \pm 2i\)

\(z^4 + 6z^2 + 25 = (z^2 - 2z + 5)(z^2 + 2z + 5)\), so \(z = -1 \pm 2i\).

State average 1.1 / 2 (55%); 42% scored full marks.
