Your Specialist progress

Course map

Specialist assumes Methods Units 3 and 4 (studied before or alongside). Calculus is the biggest area: do the techniques before differential equations and kinematics. Statistics builds on Methods' normal distribution and only needs the linear-combination rules from here.
