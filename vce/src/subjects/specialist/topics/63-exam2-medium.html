2023 VCAA Exam 2 · Section A · Question 2 Open in the exam PDF (p. 2) ↗

Values of a, b, c so that \(y = \frac{x^3}{ax^2 + bx + c}\) has asymptotes \(y = 2x + 1\) and \(x = 1\)

For large \(x\), \(y \approx \frac{x}{a} - \frac{b}{a^2}\), so \(a = \frac12\), \(b = -\frac14\). The denominator is zero at \(x = 1\): \(a + b + c = 0\), so \(c = -\frac14\).

59% of Victorian students got this right. Choices: A 10% · B 59% · C 13% · D 14% · E 2%.

2023 VCAA Exam 2 · Section A · Question 4 Open in the exam PDF (p. 3) ↗

Simplify \(\frac{4a}{1 + \bar z}\) for \(z = -(2a + 1) + 2ai\) into polar form

\(1 + \bar z = -2a(1 + i)\), so the expression is \(\frac{-2}{1 + i} = -1 + i = \sqrt2\operatorname{cis}\frac{3\pi}{4}\).

62% of Victorian students got this right. Choices: A 11% · B 62% · C 6% · D 17% · E 4%. The most popular wrong answer was D (17%).

Slope of a parametric path at a given time

\(\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{dy/dt}{dx/dt} = \frac{1/2}{2/3} = \frac34\) at \(t = 2\).

2023 VCAA Exam 2 · Section A · Question 12 Open in the exam PDF (p. 6) ↗

Velocity at a given time when \(a = 1 + v\), starting from rest

\(\frac{dv}{dt} = 1 + v \Rightarrow v = e^t - 1\). At \(t = \log_e(e + 1)\), \(v = e\).

2025 VCAA Exam 2 · Section A · Question 3 Open in the exam PDF (p. 3) ↗

Constants a, b, c from an oblique asymptote and y-intercept of \(\frac{x^2 + a}{bx + c}\)

Oblique asymptote \(y = \frac xb - \frac{c}{b^2}\): \(b = -2\), \(c = -1\). The y-intercept \(\frac ac = -2\) gives \(a = 2\).
