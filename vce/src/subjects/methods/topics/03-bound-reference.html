  • \(\left(a, b\right) = \left(\frac{1 - 2\sqrt7}{3}, \frac{1 + \sqrt7}{3}\right)\) or \(\left(\frac{1 + 2\sqrt7}{3}, \frac{1 - \sqrt7}{3}\right)\)

Limits, continuity & differentiability

Hardest Exam 2 multiple-choice question · revise the topic

2023 VCAA Exam 2 · Section A · Question 9 Open in the exam PDF (p. 5) ↗

Value of a that makes a tan/sin hybrid continuous and smooth at x = 2π

42% correct

Answer and marking points

Answer: C · A 7% · B 9% · C 42% · D 31% · E 10%

Continuity needs \(\sin(2\pi a) = 0\), so \(a = \frac n2\) for an integer \(n\). Smoothness needs \(a\cos(2\pi a) = \frac12\) (the gradient of \(\tan\frac x2\) at \(2\pi\) is \(\frac12\sec^2\pi = \frac12\)), i.e. \(\frac n2(-1)^n = \frac12\), which holds only for \(n = -1\). So \(a = -\frac12\) (\(a = \frac12\) gives \(-\frac12\)).
