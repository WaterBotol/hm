Course map

Unit 3 is data analysis and recursion and financial modelling; Unit 4 is matrices and networks and decision mathematics. Data analysis is the biggest area (40% of both exams), so it's worth the most revision time. Recursion underpins all the finance topics, so do it before loans and annuities.
