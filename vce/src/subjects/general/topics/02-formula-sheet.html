VCAA provides the same formula sheet in both General Maths exams. It's short: the cards tagged on the sheet below are on it; everything else you need to know (or put in your bound reference). Check the current official sheet on the VCAA website before your exam.

Data analysis

Standardised scores

\[ z = \frac{x - \bar x}{s_x} \]

On the sheet. \(x = \bar x + z s_x\). Normal model

The 68–95–99.7% rule

68%, 95% and 99.7% within 1, 2 and 3 standard deviations of the mean. Bands: 34%, 13.5%, 2.35%, 0.15%. Not on the sheet. Normal model

Boxplot fences

\[ Q_1 - 1.5 \times \text{IQR} \qquad Q_3 + 1.5 \times \text{IQR} \]

On the sheet. \(\text{IQR} = Q_3 - Q_1\); range = max − min. Summary statistics

Least squares line

\[ y = a + bx,\quad b = r\frac{s_y}{s_x},\quad a = \bar y - b\bar x \]

On the sheet. Passes through \((\bar x, \bar y)\). Least squares

Residuals

\[ \text{residual} = \text{actual} - \text{predicted} \]

On the sheet. \(r^2\) = proportion of variation explained; \(|r| \ge 0.75\) strong, \(0.5\)–\(0.75\) moderate, \(0.25\)–\(0.5\) weak (a common guide, not on the sheet). Residuals

Seasonal indices

\[ \text{seasonal index} = \frac{\text{actual}}{\text{deseasonalised}} \]

The index formula is on the sheet; the rearrangements aren't: deseasonalised = actual ÷ index, actual = deseasonalised × index. Indices add to the number of seasons. Seasonal indices

Smoothing

Odd \(n\): mean of \(n\) consecutive values at the middle time. Even \(n\): average two neighbouring \(n\)-point means to centre. Medians: odd \(n\) only. Not on the sheet. Time series

Transformations

Circle of transformations: \(x^2\) or \(y^2\) for curves bending one way, \(\log\) or reciprocal for the other. Pick the one that straightens the plot and leaves random residuals. Not on the sheet. Transformations

Recursion and financial modelling

Recurrence relations

\[ u_0 = a,\qquad u_{n+1} = Ru_n + d \]

On the sheet. Compound interest \(d = 0\); loan/annuity \(d < 0\); investment \(d > 0\). Recurrence

Effective interest rate

\[ r_{\text{effective}} = \left[\left(1 + \frac{r}{100n}\right)^n - 1\right] \times 100\% \]

On the sheet. More compounding periods per year means a higher effective rate. Interest

Interest and depreciation

\[ \text{simple: } d = \tfrac{r}{100}V_0 \qquad \text{compound: } R = 1 + \tfrac{r}{100k} \]\[ \text{reducing balance: } R = 1 - \tfrac{r}{100} \qquad \text{flat rate, unit cost: } R = 1,\ d = -(\text{loss per year}) \]

Not on the sheet. Depreciation

Finance Solver signs

Loan: PV +, Pmt −, FV 0. Investment: PV −, Pmt −, FV +. Annuity: PV −, Pmt +, FV 0. Not on the sheet. Finance Solver

Perpetuities

\[ d = \frac{r}{100k}V_0 \qquad V_0 = d \div \frac{r}{100k} \]

Not on the sheet. Annuities

Matrices

Determinant and inverse

\[ \det A = ad - bc \qquad A^{-1} = \frac{1}{\det A}\begin{bmatrix} d & -b \\ -c & a \end{bmatrix} \]

On the sheet. No inverse if \(\det A = 0\). \(AX = B \Rightarrow X = A^{-1}B\). Inverses

Transition, Leslie and culling

\[ S_{n+1} = TS_n + B \qquad S_{n+1} = LS_n \]

The recurrences are on the sheet. Not on it: \(S_n = T^nS_0\) (only without \(B\)); columns of \(T\) add to 1; Leslie birth rates in row 1, survival rates below the diagonal. Transition · Leslie

Matrix arithmetic

\((m \times n)(n \times p) = (m \times p)\): inner numbers must match. \(AB \ne BA\) in general. Not on the sheet. Matrix arithmetic

Communication and dominance

\(C^2\): two-step links. Rank with row sums of \(D\), then \(D + D^2\). \(PA\) reorders rows, \(AP\) reorders columns. Not on the sheet. Binary matrices

Networks and decision mathematics

Euler's formula

\[ v + f = e + 2 \]

On the sheet. Connected planar graphs only; count the outside face. Graphs

Graph facts

Sum of degrees = \(2e\). \(K_n\) has \(\frac{n(n-1)}{2}\) edges. A tree with \(n\) vertices has \(n - 1\) edges. Not on the sheet. Trees

Eulerian and Hamiltonian

Eulerian circuit: all degrees even. Eulerian trail: exactly two odd vertices (start and end there). Hamiltonian: every vertex once, no rule. Not on the sheet. Travel

Flow

Cut capacity: only edges from the source side to the sink side. Maximum flow = minimum cut. Not on the sheet. Flow

Algorithms

Prim: cheapest edge to a new vertex. Dijkstra: make the smallest label permanent, update neighbours. Hungarian: reduce rows, columns; cover zeros; adjust; allocate. Not on the sheet. Dijkstra · Hungarian

Critical path

EST = max of predecessors' (EST + duration); LST = min of followers' LST − duration; float = LST − EST. Critical path = longest path (zero float). Not on the sheet. Critical path
