Exam 1 is 40 multiple-choice questions (options A–D) in 1 hour 30 minutes, with CAS and your bound reference. This set is half an exam in the same proportions: 8 data analysis, 4 recursion and finance, 4 matrices and 4 networks. Allow about 45 minutes. Each wrong option matches a common error, so check your answer isn't the trap.

Data analysis

Q1. A survey records each person's postcode. Postcode is

  1. numerical discrete
  2. numerical continuous
  3. categorical nominal
  4. categorical ordinal

A postcode is a label for a place: arithmetic on it means nothing, and there's no natural order. Numbers can be categorical.

Q2. For the data 12, 15, 17, 18, 20, 22, 25, 26, 41, the upper fence is

  1. 39.75
  2. 38.5
  3. 25.5
  4. 40.25

Median 20. \(Q_1\) = median of 12, 15, 17, 18 = 16; \(Q_3\) = median of 22, 25, 26, 41 = 25.5. IQR = 9.5, so the upper fence is \(25.5 + 1.5 \times 9.5 = 39.75\) (and 41 is an outlier).

Q3. Adult heights are approximately normal with mean 170 cm and standard deviation 8 cm. The percentage of adults taller than 186 cm is closest to

  1. 5%
  2. 2.5%
  3. 16%
  4. 0.15%

186 is 2 standard deviations above the mean: \(\frac{100 - 95}{2} = 2.5\%\) lie above it.

Q4. A test has mean 65 and standard deviation 4. A score of 59 has a standardised score of

  1. −6
  2. 1.5
  3. −0.67
  4. −1.5

\(z = \frac{59 - 65}{4} = -1.5\).

Q5. For two variables, \(r = 0.8\), \(\bar x = 10\), \(s_x = 2\), \(\bar y = 40\) and \(s_y = 5\). The least squares line is

  1. \(y = 20 + 2x\)
  2. \(y = 36.8 + 0.32x\)
  3. \(y = 40 + 2x\)
  4. \(y = 20 + 2.5x\)

\(b = 0.8 \times \frac52 = 2\), \(a = 40 - 2 \times 10 = 20\). B uses \(\frac{s_x}{s_y}\); D forgets \(r\).

Q6. The correlation between hours of screen time (explanatory) and hours of sleep is \(r = -0.9\). Which statement is correct?

  1. 90% of the variation in sleep is explained by screen time
  2. −81% of the variation in sleep is explained by screen time
  3. 81% of the variation in sleep is explained by the variation in screen time
  4. More screen time causes less sleep

\(r^2 = 0.81\), and it's never negative. Correlation doesn't show causation.

Q7. The least squares line is \(y = 3.2 + 1.5x\). For the data point \((10, 17)\), the residual is

  1. 1.2
  2. −1.2
  3. 18.2
  4. −0.8

Predicted \(3.2 + 15 = 18.2\); residual \(= 17 - 18.2 = -1.2\) (actual − predicted).

Q8. Quarterly sales were 20, 28, 35, 25 and 23 (Q1 to Q5). The 4-point centred moving mean for Q3 is

  1. 27
  2. 27.75
  3. 35
  4. 27.375

\(\frac{20 + 28 + 35 + 25}{4} = 27\) and \(\frac{28 + 35 + 25 + 23}{4} = 27.75\) sit either side of Q3; centre them: \(\frac{27 + 27.75}{2} = 27.375\).

Recursion and financial modelling

Q9. \(V_0 = 5000\), \(V_{n+1} = 1.02V_n - 200\). The value of \(V_2\) is

  1. 4900
  2. 4600
  3. 4798
  4. 5202

\(V_1 = 5100 - 200 = 4900\); \(V_2 = 4998 - 200 = 4798\).

Q10. The effective annual interest rate for 6% per annum compounding monthly is closest to

  1. 6.00%
  2. 6.17%
  3. 6.50%
  4. 0.50%

\(\left[(1 + \frac{6}{1200})^{12} - 1\right] \times 100\% = 6.17\%\).

Q11. A $30 000 machine depreciates by 15% of its value each year. After 3 years its value is closest to

  1. $18 424
  2. $16 500
  3. $25 500
  4. $13 500

Reducing balance: \(30\,000 \times 0.85^3 = 18\,423.75\). B is flat rate (15% of the purchase price each year).

Q12. A $250 000 loan at 5.4% per annum compounding monthly is repaid with monthly payments of $1520.32 over 25 years. The interest paid in the first year is closest to

  1. $13 500.00
  2. $18 243.84
  3. $4863.03
  4. $13 380.81

After 12 payments (N = 12, I% = 5.4, PV = 250 000, Pmt = −1520.32): FV = −245 136.97, so the principal repaid is 4863.03. Interest = \(12 \times 1520.32 - 4863.03 = 13\,380.81\).

Matrices

Q13. \(A\) is a 2 × 3 matrix and \(B\) is a 3 × 4 matrix. Which statement is true?

  1. \(BA\) is a 4 × 2 matrix
  2. \(AB\) is a 2 × 4 matrix
  3. \(A + B\) is defined
  4. \(AB\) is a 3 × 3 matrix

\((2 \times 3)(3 \times 4) = 2 \times 4\). \(BA\) needs \(4 = 2\), so it isn't defined.

Q14. \(\begin{bmatrix} 3 & 2 \\ 4 & k \end{bmatrix}\) has no inverse when \(k\) equals

  1. 0
  2. \(-\frac83\)
  3. \(\frac83\)
  4. 6

\(\det = 3k - 8 = 0\), so \(k = \frac83\).

Q15. \(T = \begin{bmatrix} 0.7 & 0.4 \\ 0.3 & 0.6 \end{bmatrix}\) (columns this week, rows next week) and \(S_0 = \begin{bmatrix} 200 \\ 300 \end{bmatrix}\). Then \(S_1\) is

  1. \(\begin{bmatrix} 260 \\ 240 \end{bmatrix}\)
  2. \(\begin{bmatrix} 230 \\ 260 \end{bmatrix}\)
  3. \(\begin{bmatrix} 140 \\ 180 \end{bmatrix}\)
  4. \(\begin{bmatrix} 300 \\ 200 \end{bmatrix}\)

\(0.7(200) + 0.4(300) = 260\); \(0.3(200) + 0.6(300) = 240\). B uses the transpose of \(T\).

Q16. A Leslie matrix is \(\begin{bmatrix} 0 & 2 & 1 \\ 0.5 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0.4 & 0 \end{bmatrix}\) and the initial state is 100, 60 and 40 females in age groups 1, 2 and 3. After one period, age group 1 contains

  1. 50 females
  2. 100 females
  3. 120 females
  4. 160 females

Row 1 holds the birth rates: \(2(60) + 1(40) = 160\). (C counts only age group 2's births; A is the survivors into group 2.)

Networks and decision mathematics

Q17. A connected planar graph has 7 vertices and 11 edges. The number of faces is

  1. 4
  2. 6
  3. 16
  4. 20

\(7 + f = 11 + 2\), so \(f = 6\).

Q18. Which list of vertex degrees, for a connected graph, guarantees an Eulerian circuit?

  1. 2, 4, 2, 4, 6
  2. 2, 3, 3, 4
  3. 1, 2, 3, 4, 4
  4. 3, 3, 3, 3

An Eulerian circuit needs every degree even. B has two odd vertices (a trail only).

Q19. A cut crosses edges of capacity 5 and 8 going from the source side to the sink side, and one edge of capacity 3 going from the sink side to the source side. The capacity of the cut is

  1. 16
  2. 10
  3. 13
  4. 5

Only edges from the source side to the sink side count: \(5 + 8 = 13\).

Q20. Activity D has an earliest starting time of 6 days and a duration of 3 days. Its only following activity has a latest starting time of 12 days. The float of D is

  1. 0 days
  2. 6 days
  3. 9 days
  4. 3 days

LST of D \(= 12 - 3 = 9\). Float \(= 9 - 6 = 3\) days.

Summary
