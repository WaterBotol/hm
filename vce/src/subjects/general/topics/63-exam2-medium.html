General Mathematics Exam 2 · real exam questions, medium

Every question here is a real VCAA exam question. They were chosen by ranking every question from the 2023–2025 exams by how Victorian students actually scored on it (state average ÷ marks), within each area of study, so the paper keeps the real exam’s balance of about 24 marks of data analysis and 12 each of the other three areas. This paper takes the middle band. The questions themselves are in the official PDFs (VCAA copyright), so open each one with its link. Answer here, then mark yourself against the guide.

How the state went on these exact questions: an average of 31 / 60 (52%). Beat that.

Data analysis

2023 VCAA Exam 2 · Question 1 Open in the exam PDF (p. 2) ↗

Oyster data (weight, volume, image size, size) for 15 oysters: summary statistics, a least squares line and the normal model

State average for the whole question: 4.6 / 9 (51%).

Number of categorical variables in the table

  • 2 (ID and size)

2. ID is a label and size is small/medium/large. Half the state missed it.

State average 0.5 / 1 (50%); 50% scored full marks.

Mark distribution: 0: 50% · 1: 50%

Mean weight of all 15 oysters

  • 11.42 g (exact; don’t round)

11.42 g. Rounding to 11.4 lost the mark: no rounding instruction was given.

State average 0.9 / 1 (90%); 86% scored full marks.

Mark distribution: 0: 14% · 1: 86%

Median weight of the large oysters only

  • 14.1 g

14.1 g. Many gave the median of the whole data set instead.

State average 0.4 / 1 (40%); 39% scored full marks.

Mark distribution: 0: 61% · 1: 39%

Response variable in the given volume–weight equation

  • volume

Volume (the variable on the left of the equation).

State average 0.9 / 1 (90%); 88% scored full marks.

Mark distribution: 0: 12% · 1: 88%

Predicted change in volume for a 10 g increase in weight

  • 9.53 cm³

10 × 0.953 = 9.53 cm³. A common error was 0.953 (one gram, not ten).

State average 0.3 / 1 (30%); 27% scored full marks.

Mark distribution: 0: 73% · 1: 27%

Least squares line for volume against image size, coefficients to four significant figures

  • Correct coefficients: 0.002857 and 2.571
  • Correct placement: volume = 0.002857 + 2.571 × image size

volume = 0.002857 + 2.571 × image size. Examiners saw coefficients wrongly rounded and variables swapped.

State average 1 / 2 (50%); 37% scored full marks.

Mark distribution: 0: 38% · 1: 26% · 2: 37%

Mean and standard deviation of megapixels from two percentages, using the 68–95–99.7% rule

  • Set up μ + 2σ = 4.6 and μ − σ = 4.3
  • Mean 4.4, standard deviation 0.1

97.5% below 4.6 means 4.6 = μ + 2σ; 84% above 4.3 means 4.3 = μ − σ. So σ = 0.1 and μ = 4.4.

State average 0.6 / 2 (30%); 29% scored full marks.

Mark distribution: 0: 67% · 1: 3% · 2: 29%

2024 VCAA Exam 2 · Question 1 Open in the exam PDF (p. 2) ↗

Men’s Olympic high jump gold medal heights (Mgold), 1928–2020: summary statistics, z-score, boxplot and a least squares line against year

State average for the whole question: 5.9 / 8 (74%).

Maximum Mgold

  • 2.39 m

2.39 m.

State average 1 / 1 (100%); 96% scored full marks.

Mark distribution: 0: 4% · 1: 96%

Percentage of Mgold values greater than 2.25 m

  • 50%

50%. The most common wrong answer, 54.54%, included 2.25 itself.

State average 0.8 / 1 (80%); 77% scored full marks.

Mark distribution: 0: 23% · 1: 77%

Standardised score for the 2000 height of 2.35 m (mean 2.23, sd 0.15)

  • z = 0.8

(2.35 − 2.23) ÷ 0.15 = 0.8.

State average 0.9 / 1 (90%); 84% scored full marks.

Mark distribution: 0: 16% · 1: 84%

Construct a boxplot of Mgold on a grid

  • Box from 2.12 to 2.36 with median 2.29
  • Whiskers to 1.94 and 2.39

Five-number summary 1.94, 2.12, 2.29, 2.36, 2.39. A complete boxplot with three correct values earned one mark.

State average 1.7 / 2 (85%); 76% scored full marks.

Mark distribution: 0: 6% · 1: 17% · 2: 76%

Least squares line for Mgold against year, coefficients to three significant figures

  • Intercept −7.97
  • Slope 0.00516

Mgold = −7.97 + 0.00516 × year. Many couldn’t read CAS exponent notation (5.16E−3).

State average 1 / 2 (50%); 39% scored full marks.

Mark distribution: 0: 35% · 1: 26% · 2: 39%

Interpret r² = 0.857

  • 85.7% of the variation in Mgold can be explained by the variation in year

Mention both variables, and don’t round (86% lost the mark).

State average 0.5 / 1 (50%); 52% scored full marks.

Mark distribution: 0: 48% · 1: 52%

Apartment sale price against days on the market for 10 apartments

Least squares line, both values to four significant figures

  • 1 050 000
  • −8050

sale price = 1 050 000 − 8050 × days. CAS shows 1.05E6: read exponent notation carefully.

  • 75% of the variation in sale price can be explained by the variation in days

2025 VCAA Exam 2 · Question 6 Open in the exam PDF (p. 9) ↗

Monthly home sales over four years: qualitative features and a seasonal index for September

State average for the whole question: 1.5 / 4 (38%).

Two qualitative features of the time series (excluding outliers)

  • seasonality
  • irregular fluctuations

Seasonality and irregular fluctuations; there’s no trend.

State average 1.1 / 2 (55%); 32% scored full marks.

Mark distribution: 0: 23% · 1: 45% · 2: 32%

Seasonal index for September to three decimal places, from yearly totals

  • Each year: September value ÷ (yearly total ÷ 12)
  • Average of the four proportions = 0.588

15 ÷ 30.083, 20 ÷ 29.5, 20 ÷ 29.833 and 15 ÷ 29.75, then average: 0.588. Only 12% got both marks.

State average 0.4 / 2 (20%); 12% scored full marks.

Mark distribution: 0: 74% · 1: 14% · 2: 12%

A $600 000 annuity paying $3973 per month: interest rate, amortisation table and recurrence relation

Annual interest rate from 0.42% per month

  • 5.04%

Complete the next line of the amortisation table

  • 3973.00, 2507.77, 1465.23, 595 622.67

State average 0.4 / 1 (40%); 40% scored full marks.

Mark distribution: 0: 60% · 1: 40%

Recurrence relation for the annuity balance

  • V₀ = 600 000, Vₙ₊₁ = 1.0042Vₙ − 3973

V₀ = 600 000, Vₙ₊₁ = 1.0042Vₙ − 3973.

State average 0.4 / 1 (40%); 41% scored full marks.

Mark distribution: 0: 59% · 1: 41%

Name for an annuity whose balance stays constant

  • perpetuity

A perpetuity.

State average 0.6 / 1 (60%); 59% scored full marks.

Mark distribution: 0: 41% · 1: 59%

A communication matrix G between circus groups (directors, managers, performers, sales staff, customers)

Customers reach sales staff, sales staff reach managers, managers reach directors.

  • All 16 missing elements correct (H = G²)

How Victoria went on this paper

These statistics are the real published results for each question in the year it was set, compiled from the VCAA examiners’ reports. A student scoring at the state average on every question would get 31 / 60 (52%) on this paper.

Where Victorian students lost the most marks

  • 2025 Exam 2 Q6 (Monthly home sales over four years: qualitative features and a seasonal index for September): 38% of available marks
  • 2023 Exam 2 Q8 (Circus ticket prices in a column matrix N (family, adult, child)): 40% of available marks
  • 2023 Exam 2 Q13 (Roads between nine landmarks: shortest path, a Hamiltonian cycle and repeating roads to travel every road): 40% of available marks
  • 2025 Exam 2 Q18 (A 12-activity home gym project: two critical paths, latest start time, float and crashing): 42% of available marks
  • 2024 Exam 2 Q11 (A Leslie matrix model of a native animal’s female population in four age groups): 43% of available marks
  • 2024 Exam 2 Q14 (Deliveries from a manufacturer to a supermarket through warehouses: cut capacity, maximum flow and the best edge to increase): 43% of available marks

What most students got right

  • 2024 Exam 2 Q1 (Men’s Olympic high jump gold medal heights (Mgold), 1928–2020: summary statistics, z-score, boxplot and a least squares line against year): 74% of available marks

  • 2023 Exam 2 Q10 (A communication matrix G between circus groups (directors, managers, performers, sales staff, customers)): 57% of available marks
