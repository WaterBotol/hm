General Mathematics Exam 1 · real exam questions, medium

Every question here is a real VCAA exam question. They were chosen by ranking every question from the 2023–2025 exams by how Victorian students actually scored on it (percentage correct), within each area of study, so the paper keeps the real exam’s balance of 16 data analysis, 8 recursion and finance, 8 matrices and 8 networks questions. This paper takes the middle band. The questions themselves are in the official PDFs (VCAA copyright), so open each one with its link. Answer here, then mark yourself against the guide.

How the state went on these exact questions: an average of 27 / 40 (67%). Beat that.

Data analysis

2023 VCAA Exam 1 · Question 12 Open in the exam PDF (p. 9) ↗

Age of a tree from a log₁₀(age) model and a given height

log₁₀(age) = (8.52 + 3.8) ÷ 12.6 = 0.978, so age = 10^0.978 ≈ 9.5 years.

76% of Victorian students got this right. Choices: A 10% · B 6% · C 4% · D 76% · E 4%.

Reducing balance rate for the same computer

3000 × R⁴ = 600 gives R = 0.2^0.25 ≈ 0.669, a rate of about 33%.

48% of Victorian students got this right. Choices: A 7% · B 14% · C 20% · D 11% · E 48%.

Total interest on a five-year loan with given monthly repayments

Total repaid = 2228.40 × 60 = 133 704; interest = 133 704 − 121 000 = $12 704.

56% of Victorian students got this right. Choices: A 12% · B 15% · C 16% · D 56%.

2024 VCAA Exam 1 · Question 23 Open in the exam PDF (p. 13) ↗

Time to repay the same loan

I% = 0.4 × 12 = 4.8, PV = 240 000, Pmt = −2741.05, FV = 0: N = 108 months, which is 9 years.

54% of Victorian students got this right. Choices: A 54% · B 13% · C 18% · D 14%.

2025 VCAA Exam 1 · Question 27 Open in the exam PDF (p. 13) ↗

Which pair of values of m and n makes a 2 × 2 matrix singular

det = mn + 36 must not be 0, so mn ≠ −36: 3 and −12 is not allowed.

76% of Victorian students got this right. Choices: A 4% · B 6% · C 76% · D 13%.

2025 VCAA Exam 1 · Question 30 Open in the exam PDF (p. 16) ↗

Number of negative elements when fᵢⱼ = i² − j in a 4 × 4 matrix

Only row 1 has negatives (j = 2, 3, 4). Row 2 gives 4 − j ≥ 0. Three elements.

73% of Victorian students got this right. Choices: A 10% · B 73% · C 12% · D 5%.

2025 VCAA Exam 1 · Question 31 Open in the exam PDF (p. 16) ↗

Which matrix computation is defined, given four matrices of different orders

A is 2 × 2, B 3 × 1, C 3 × 2, D 1 × 3. Cᵀ(2 × 3) B(3 × 1) is 2 × 1, and times D(1 × 3) gives 2 × 3. The others fail.

70% of Victorian students got this right. Choices: A 5% · B 70% · C 14% · D 10%.

2025 VCAA Exam 1 · Question 32 Open in the exam PDF (p. 17) ↗

Complete a partial dominance matrix from match information and find T = D + D²

Kyle’s two wins are Maggie and Neil, so Lian beat Kyle. Lian’s only win is Kyle, so Neil beat Lian. Complete D, compute D² and add: matrix B.

63% of Victorian students got this right. Choices: A 10% · B 63% · C 6% · D 21%.

Weight of the minimum spanning tree of a small weighted graph

Use Prim’s algorithm: the cheapest connecting edges total 40.

74% of Victorian students got this right. Choices: A 3% · B 3% · C 74% · D 12% · E 7%.

2023 VCAA Exam 1 · Question 37 Open in the exam PDF (p. 22) ↗

Number of faces of a planar graph given its adjacency matrix

The matrix total is 20, so there are 10 edges. Euler: 5 + f = 10 + 2, so f = 7.

66% of Victorian students got this right. Choices: A 7% · B 66% · C 14% · D 5% · E 7%.

2023 VCAA Exam 1 · Question 38 Open in the exam PDF (p. 23) ↗

Which activity network (with a dummy) matches a precedence table

F and I both follow D and E, so D and E end at one vertex where F and I start. G needs C and F but H needs only F, so a dummy joins the end of F to the start of G. Only option D shows exactly this.

60% of Victorian students got this right. Choices: A 13% · B 10% · C 10% · D 60% · E 6%.

2023 VCAA Exam 1 · Question 39 Open in the exam PDF (p. 24) ↗

Capacity of the minimum cut of a maze flow network

Check cuts from entrance to exit, counting only edges from the entrance side to the exit side: the smallest is 23.

62% of Victorian students got this right. Choices: A 6% · B 62% · C 12% · D 6% · E 14%.

2025 VCAA Exam 1 · Question 35 Open in the exam PDF (p. 19) ↗

Length of a minimum spanning tree that contains an edge of unknown weight w

Prim’s algorithm on the other edges, plus w: 5 + 6 + 4 + 2 + 4 + 5 + 3 + 6 + 5 + 4 + w = 44 + w.

65% of Victorian students got this right. Choices: A 8% · B 65% · C 15% · D 12%.

2025 VCAA Exam 1 · Question 37 Open in the exam PDF (p. 20) ↗

Maximum flow from source to sink in the same network

The minimum cut is through the three pipes into the sink: 7 + 4 + 6 = 17.

73% of Victorian students got this right. Choices: A 13% · B 73% · C 10% · D 3%.

Table after row and column reduction (Hungarian algorithm) of a contractor cost table

Row minimums 9600, 10 000, 12 400, 9000, then column minimums 4500, 0, 13 150, 8700: table B.
