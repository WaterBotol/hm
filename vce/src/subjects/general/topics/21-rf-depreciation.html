Three ways an asset loses value

Cars, computers and machinery lose value (depreciate) over time. The current value is called the book value; when it reaches a minimum useful value it is the scrap value.

MethodLoses each periodRecurrence relationRule
Flat ratea fixed amount \(d\) (often \(r\%\) of the purchase price)\(V_0 = \text{price},\ V_{n+1} = V_n - d\)\(V_n = V_0 - nd\)
Unit costcost per unit × units used that period\(V_{n+1} = V_n - d\), \(d = \text{rate} \times \text{units}\)\(V_n = V_0 - nd\)
Reducing balance\(r\%\) of the current value\(V_{n+1} = R\,V_n,\ R = 1 - \frac{r}{100}\)\(V_n = R^n V_0\)

Flat rate 20% means 20% of the original price every year, so the same dollar amount each year. Reducing balance 20% means 20% of this year's value, so the dollar amount shrinks every year. Read which one the question says.

Flat rate and unit cost

A ute bought for \$40 000 depreciates at a flat rate of 15% of its purchase price per year. Write a recurrence relation and find its value after 4 years.

Flat rate: a fixed amount each year, worked out from the purchase price.

\(d = 0.15 \times 40\,000 = 6000\). Recurrence: \(V_0 = 40\,000\), \(V_{n+1} = V_n - 6000\).

\(V_4 = 40\,000 - 4 \times 6000 = 16\,000\).

\$16 000 after 4 years.

A delivery van worth \$38 000 depreciates by \$0.25 for every kilometre travelled. It travels 20 000 km each year. Find the annual depreciation and its value after 3 years. After how many kilometres will it be worth \$10 000?

Unit cost: depreciation = cost per unit × number of units.

Annual depreciation: \(0.25 \times 20\,000 = \$5000\). After 3 years: \(38\,000 - 3 \times 5000 = \$23\,000\).

Total drop to \$10 000 is \$28 000, which takes \(\frac{28\,000}{0.25} = 112\,000\) km.

\$5000 per year; \$23 000 after 3 years; worth \$10 000 after 112 000 km.

Reducing balance

The same \$40 000 ute instead depreciates by 20% of its current value each year. (a) Find its value after 4 years. (b) After how many whole years is it first worth less than \$10 000?

Reducing balance is geometric decay with \(R = 1 - 0.2 = 0.8\).

(a) \(V_4 = 0.8^4 \times 40\,000 = 16\,384\).

(b) Generate terms (\(V_5 = 13\,107.20\), \(V_6 = 10\,485.76\), \(V_7 = 8388.61\)) or solve \(0.8^n \times 40\,000 < 10\,000\) on CAS (\(n > 6.2\)).

(a) \$16 384. (b) After 7 years.

Reducing balance loses more in the early years (20% of \$40 000 = \$8000 in year 1, versus \$6000 flat rate) but less later. The two value curves cross: here the flat-rate value is lower from year 4 onwards. To find when one method gives a lower value, generate both sequences side by side on your CAS.

Practice

A laptop costing \$2400 depreciates at a flat rate of \$480 per year. Its value after 3 years is

  1. \$1440
  2. \$960
  3. \$1229
  4. \$480

\(2400 - 3 \times 480 = 960\).

A machine depreciates by 12% of its current value each year. The recurrence relation is

  1. \(V_{n+1} = V_n - 0.12\)
  2. \(V_{n+1} = 1.12V_n\)
  3. \(V_{n+1} = 0.12V_n\)
  4. \(V_{n+1} = 0.88V_n\)

Reducing balance: \(R = 1 - 0.12 = 0.88\).

A printer depreciates by \$0.02 per page printed. It was bought for \$900 and prints 6000 pages a year. Its value after 2 years is

  1. \$660
  2. \$780
  3. \$899.76
  4. \$240

\(0.02 \times 6000 = \$120\) per year, so \(900 - 2 \times 120 = 660\).

A tractor's value is given by \(V_n = 85\,000 \times 0.9^n\). The depreciation during the third year is closest to

  1. \$8500
  2. \$61 965
  3. \$6885
  4. \$7650

\(V_2 = 68\,850\) and \(V_3 = 61\,965\); the drop during year 3 is \(68\,850 - 61\,965 = 6885\).

A coffee machine bought for \$6500 depreciates at a flat rate of 18% of its purchase price each year. Write the rule for its value after \(n\) years and find its value after 5 years.

\(d = 0.18 \times 6500 = 1170\), so \(V_n = 6500 - 1170n\). [1]

\(V_5 = 6500 - 5850 = \$650\). [1]

Equipment worth \$24 000 can be depreciated either at a flat rate of \$3000 per year or by reducing balance at 16% per year. (a) Find the value after 3 years by each method. (b) Which method first gives the lower value, and from which year?

(a) Flat rate: \(24\,000 - 9000 = \$15\,000\). [1] Reducing balance: \(0.84^3 \times 24\,000 = \$14\,224.90\). [1]

(b) Year 1: \$21 000 vs \$20 160, so reducing balance is lower from the first year. (It stays lower up to year 4; from year 5 the flat-rate value is lower.) [1]

Summary

  • \(V_{n+1} = V_n - d\), \(V_n = V_0 - nd\); \(d\) is a fixed amount (often a % of the purchase price).
  • Depreciation = cost per unit × units used (e.g. dollars per km × km travelled).
  • \(V_{n+1} = RV_n\), \(V_n = R^nV_0\), with \(R = 1 - \frac{r}{100}\).
  • Flat rate: a straight line (constant drop). Reducing balance: a curve, dropping less each year and never reaching zero.
