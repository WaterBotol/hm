General Mathematics Exam 1 · real exam questions, easier

Every question here is a real VCAA exam question. They were chosen by ranking every question from the 2023–2025 exams by how Victorian students actually scored on it (percentage correct), within each area of study, so the paper keeps the real exam’s balance of 16 data analysis, 8 recursion and finance, 8 matrices and 8 networks questions. This paper takes the highest band. The questions themselves are in the official PDFs (VCAA copyright), so open each one with its link. Answer here, then mark yourself against the guide.

2023 VCAA Exam 1 · Question 1 Open in the exam PDF (p. 3) ↗

Median from a dot plot of 40 runners’ 800 m times

With 40 values the median is the mean of the 20th and 21st values; counting up the dot plot gives 136 s.

90% of Victorian students got this right. Choices: A 1% · B 90% · C 6% · D 2% · E 1%.

2025 VCAA Exam 1 · Question 5 Open in the exam PDF (p. 4) ↗

Mean and standard deviation from two percentages of a normal distribution

2.5% above 178.9 means 178.9 = μ + 2σ; 16% below 157.6 means 157.6 = μ − σ. So 3σ = 21.3, σ = 7.1 and μ = 164.7.

81% of Victorian students got this right. Choices: A 6% · B 10% · C 81% · D 3%.

2024 VCAA Exam 1 · Question 22 Open in the exam PDF (p. 13) ↗

Principal reduction for payment 2 in an amortisation table

Monthly rate = 960 ÷ 240 000 = 0.4%. Interest = 0.004 × 238 218.95 = 952.88; principal reduction = 2741.05 − 952.88 = $1788.17.

70% of Victorian students got this right. Choices: A 9% · B 16% · C 70% · D 5%.

2025 VCAA Exam 1 · Question 21 Open in the exam PDF (p. 11) ↗

When total perpetuity payments first exceed the amount invested

Each payment is 5% of 250 000 = 12 500, and 250 000 ÷ 12 500 = 20, so the total first exceeds it at the end of year 21.

69% of Victorian students got this right. Choices: A 8% · B 16% · C 69% · D 6%.
