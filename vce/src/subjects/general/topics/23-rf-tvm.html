The seven boxes

Every loan, annuity and investment question in General Maths can be solved with your CAS's financial solver (Finance Solver on TI-Nspire, Financial on ClassPad). Fill in six of the boxes and solve for the seventh.

BoxMeaningExample
Ntotal number of payments (or compounding periods if there are no payments)5 years of monthly payments: 60
I(%)the annual interest rate7.2
PVpresent value: the amount at the startamount borrowed or invested
Pmtthe regular paymentmonthly repayment
FVfuture value: the amount at the end0 when a loan is fully repaid
PpYpayments per year12 for monthly
CpYcompounding periods per yearusually the same as PpY

The sign convention

Think from your point of view (the borrower or investor): money that comes to you is positive; money that leaves you is negative.

SituationPVPmtFV
Reducing balance loan+ (bank gives you money)− (you repay)0 (paid off) or − (still owe)
Annuity investment (saving)− (you deposit)− (you keep depositing)+ (you get it back)
Annuity (drawing an income)− (you invest a lump sum)+ (you receive income)0 (runs out) or + (money left)
Perpetuity− (you invest)+ (you receive interest)+ (equal in size to PV: never runs down)
Compound interest, no payments− (you invest)0+

If PV and FV have the same sign for a loan, or Pmt has the wrong sign, the solver gives nonsense (or an error). Before solving, check: does money go one way at the start and the other way during the loan? For a loan, PV is positive and Pmt negative.

Solving for each unknown

Jo borrows \$25 000 at 7.2% per annum compounding monthly, to be repaid with equal monthly payments over 5 years. Find the monthly payment and the total interest paid.

A loan paid off completely: FV = 0. PV is positive (Jo receives the money).

N = 60, I(%) = 7.2, PV = 25 000, FV = 0, PpY = CpY = 12. Solve for Pmt: −497.39.

Total paid: \(60 \times 497.39 = 29\,843.40\); interest \(= 29\,843.40 - 25\,000\).

Payment \$497.39 per month; total interest \$4843.40.

How much does Jo still owe after 2 years?

Use N = 24 and the actual (rounded) payment, then solve for FV.

N = 24, I(%) = 7.2, PV = 25 000, Pmt = −497.39, PpY = CpY = 12. Solve for FV: −16 061.23 (negative: still owed).

\$16 061.23.

A \$300 000 mortgage at 6% per annum compounding monthly is repaid at \$2500 per month. How many payments are needed, and what is the final (smaller) payment?

Solve for N. A non-whole N means the last payment is smaller: find the balance just before it.

I(%) = 6, PV = 300 000, Pmt = −2500, FV = 0, PpY = CpY = 12: N = 183.7, so 184 payments.

After 183 payments (N = 183, solve FV): −1782.13. One more month of interest: \(1782.13 \times 1.005 = 1791.04\).

184 payments: 183 of \$2500 and a final payment of \$1791.04.

\$10 000 invested with interest compounding monthly grows to \$15 000 in 8 years with no extra deposits. Find the annual interest rate.

An investment with no payments: PV negative, FV positive, Pmt = 0.

N = 96, PV = −10 000, Pmt = 0, FV = 15 000, PpY = CpY = 12. Solve for I(%).

About 5.08% per annum.

For method marks, write down what you entered: “N = 60, I% = 7.2, PV = 25 000, Pmt = ?, FV = 0, PpY = CpY = 12, Pmt = −497.39”. Give money to the nearest cent, and only round when the question tells you to.

For a loan: total interest = total of all payments − amount borrowed (− any remaining balance paid off at the end). For an investment: total interest = final value − total deposits (including the initial amount).

After some payments on a loan, Finance Solver gives FV = −8200. This means

  1. the borrower has overpaid by \$8200
  2. the bank owes the borrower \$8200
  3. the borrower still owes \$8200
  4. \$8200 of interest has been paid

From the borrower's view, a negative FV is money still to be paid.

For Jo's loan (\$25 000, 7.2% p.a. monthly, \$497.39 per month): (a) how much interest is paid in the first 2 years? (b) How much of the principal has been repaid after 2 years?

Balance after 24 payments: \$16 061.23. Principal repaid: \(25\,000 - 16\,061.23 = \$8938.77\). [1]

Payments made: \(24 \times 497.39 = \$11\,937.36\). [1]

Interest in 2 years: \(11\,937.36 - 8938.77 = \$2998.59\). [1]

Summary

  • Number of payments, annual rate, present value, payment, future value, payments per year, compounding periods per year.
  • Money to you is +, money from you is −. Loan: PV +, Pmt −, FV 0 or −. Saving: PV −, Pmt −, FV +.
  • Balance: solve FV with N = payments made. Final payment: balance after the last full payment × (1 + periodic rate).
  • Total of payments − amount borrowed (accounting for any balance still owed).
